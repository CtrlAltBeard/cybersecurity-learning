#### **SIEM Query Cheat Sheet**


| **Use Case**          | **Splunk Query**                                                                           | **Description**                      |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ |
| Failed logins         | `index=auth action=failed_login | stats count by user, src_ip`                             | Count failed logins by user and IP.  |
| Brute force detection | `index=auth action=failed_login | stats count as attempts by src_ip | where attempts > 10` | Flag IPs with &gt;10 failed logins.  |
| Malware indicators    | `index=endpoint process_name="svchost.exe" parent_process="explorer.exe"`                  | Detect suspicious process execution. |
| Data exfiltration     | `index=network dest_port=443 bytes_out > 1000000 | stats sum(bytes_out) by src_ip`         | Identify large data transfers.       |

## **📌 Tips for Learning Cybersecurity**

1. **Start with the Basics**: Master networking, Linux, and Python before diving into advanced topics.
2. **Hands-On Practice**: Use platforms like TryHackMe, Hack The Box, and VulnHub to apply your knowledge.
3. **Stay Updated**: Follow cybersecurity news and blogs to stay informed about the latest threats and trends.
4. **Network**: Join communities and attend conferences to connect with other professionals.
5. **Build a Portfolio**: Document your projects and share them on GitHub or a personal website.
6. **Pursue Certifications**: Validate your skills with industry-recognized certifications.
7. **Ethical Mindset**: Always adhere to legal and ethical guidelines in your work.
